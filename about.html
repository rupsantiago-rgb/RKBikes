About RKBikes

RKBikes is a simple and fast platform to find and rent bicycles.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to make bicycle rental easier for people who want to travel around the city, enjoy outdoor activities or simply choose a different way of transportation.

Our Service

RKBikes offers different types of bicycles for different needs. Users can explore the available bikes, check their current status and choose the option that best suits their type of ride.

Why Choose RKBikes?

We focus on creating a clear and easy-to-use website. Our goal is to provide useful information without making the rental process unnecessarily complicated.

Our Vision

We want RKBikes to become a practical platform for bicycle rental, encouraging people to use bicycles as a comfortable, accessible and sustainable means of transportation.

Technology

The project combines HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP and SQL to create a functional and easy-to-use website. These technologies help us build the interface, manage user accounts and connect the website to a database.

Our Commitment

We aim to keep improving RKBikes by making the website more responsive, accessible and comfortable to use on computers, tablets and mobile devices.
